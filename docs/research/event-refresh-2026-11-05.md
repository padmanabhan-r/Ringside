## 4. Competition check for Ringside

rate card, places a PayPal authorization hold (Orders v2 `intent: AUTHORIZE`), handles a spoken mid-job change order

### What Ringside still has to itself (from the pages read)

No entry found combines (a) an **inbound phone call** answered by a voice agent, (b) a quote from a **rate card**,
(c) an Orders v2 **AUTHORIZE** hold, (d) a **spoken change order approved by the customer mid-job**, and (e) capture
of the final amount, for a **one-person trades business**. The nearest, First Answer Deposits, covers (b), (c) and (e)
for cleaners and contractors, plus text-approved "extra work". It is browser-speech, not a phone line, though its
"What's next" says "Real SMS and phone numbers (Twilio)". So the voice-on-a-real-call experience and the
mid-job change order on the existing hold are what set Ringside apart. One PayPal detail matters here: a capture
above the authorized amount is limited, so a change order larger than the allowed over-capture needs a
reauthorization or a second order. Check paypal-api-reference.md before you build it.
