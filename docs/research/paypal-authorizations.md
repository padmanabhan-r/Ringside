# PayPal authorize → change order → final capture → invoice → refund: research

Verified **2026-11-05**. Docs only, no credentialed calls. Sources: developer.paypal.com `.md` page variants
(fetched live), OpenAPI `api/payments/v2/schema.json` (v2.12), `api/orders/v2/schema.json` (v2.36),
`api/invoicing/v2/schema.json` (v2.12), `api/webhooks/v1/schema.json`, npm registry, Stack Overflow, Devpost.
Schema quotes below come from the downloaded JSON (`x-errors`, `components.schemas`, `paths`).
**[UNVERIFIED]** = could not confirm. **[DISCREPANCY]** = sources disagree, both shown.

## 1. Capturing more than the authorized amount

- Docs page: "You can capture up to 115% or $75 USD more than the original authorized amount, whichever is less." — https://developer.paypal.com/payment-methods/auth-honor
- Error catalogue, `MAX_CAPTURE_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED`: "Capture amount exceeds allowable limit. Please contact customer service or your account manager to request the change to your overage limit. The default overage limit is 115%, which allows the sum of all captures to be up to 115% of the order amount. Local regulations (e.g. in PSD2 countries) prohibit overages above the amount authorized by the payer." — https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments/v2/schema.json (`x-errors`)
- **[DISCREPANCY]** The same schema's 422 example for capture says: "Capture amount specified exceeded allowable limit. You can only capture up to the original authorization amount." (`authorizations_capture_422_amount_limit_exceeded`) — https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments/v2/schema.json. And the capture request's `amount`: "To capture a portion of the full authorized amount, specify an amount. If amount is not specified, the full authorized amount is captured." Values: 115%/$75 overage (auth-honor page, x-errors) vs. "only up to the original authorization amount" (422 example).
- **[DISCREPANCY]** Delay-capture page: "You can capture any amount up to the authorized total." — https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/delay-capture
- Help Center (PSD2 page): "The default overcharge limit is 115%, which allows the sum of all captures to be up to 115% of the authorization amount. Overcharges are not allowed for consumers from PSD2 countries" — https://www.paypal.com/va/cshelp/article/overcapture-requirements-psd2-help954 (US buyer/merchant are outside PSD2.)
- Community (Preston PHX, SO, Jan 2022): "You can also capture up to 115% of or $75 USD more than the original authorized amount, whichever is less. This applies only to captures of authorizations" — https://stackoverflow.com/questions/70879188/reauthorization-of-changed-amount-after-approval
- Account enablement: x-errors say "contact customer service or your account manager to request the change to your overage limit" (implies the default 115% applies without enablement, raising it needs contact). Whether the default applies on a new **sandbox** merchant: not stated **[UNVERIFIED]**; must test.
- Related issue names (schema `x-errors`): `MAX_CAPTURE_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED`, `MAX_CAPTURE_COUNT_EXCEEDED`, `AUTHORIZATION_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED` (authorize/reauthorize: "Authorization amount specified exceeded allowable limit…"), `AUTH_CAPTURE_CURRENCY_MISMATCH`. `AMOUNT_CANNOT_BE_GREATER_THAN_AUTHORIZED`: not in the schema.
- Odd wording, authorization status `CAPTURED`: "The authorized payment has one or more captures against it. The sum of these captured payments is greater than the amount of the original authorized payment." — https://developer.paypal.com/api/orders/v2/schema.json

## 2. Honor period, validity, reauthorize

- "PayPal places a hold on the buyer's funds for a **29-day authorization period**. Within this period, there is a **3-day honor period** … After the honor period expires, capture success depends on risk assessment and fund availability" — https://developer.paypal.com/payment-methods/auth-honor
- **[DISCREPANCY]** "In the United States, authorizations are valid for 3 days, and up to 29 days in other regions." / "After expiration, funds are automatically released to the customer." — https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/extend-authorization. vs. 29 days with a 3-day honour period (auth-honor page; Orders schema `AUTHORIZE`: "best captured within three days … available to capture for up to 29 days").
- **[DISCREPANCY]** "Authorizations expire after 3 days (standard) or 29 days (honor period)." — https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/void-authorized-payment (confuses the terms).
- Sandbox: "Sandbox authorizations don't expire after 29 days. Test authorizations with negative testing instead." — https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/delay-capture
- Expiry signal: `PAYMENT.AUTHORIZATION.VOIDED` "A payment authorization is voided either due to authorization reaching its 30 day validity period or authorization was manually voided" — https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/webhooks/event-names. Schema callback example `authorization_voided_expired`: `"summary": "A payment authorization was expired"`, `"status": "VOIDED"` — https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments/v2/schema.json
- Reauthorize timing: "You can reauthorize an authorized payment from 4 to 29 days after the 3-day honor period." and "If 30 days have transpired since the date of the original authorization, you must create an authorized payment instead" — Payments schema, `POST …/reauthorize` description.
- **[DISCREPANCY] count**: "you can issue multiple re-authorizations after the honor period expires" (reauthorize path description; also auth-honor page: "you can issue multiple reauthorizations within the 29-day authorization window") vs. "You can reauthorize a payment only once from days four to 29" / "You can reauthorize an authorized payment once" (schema `reauthorize_request`), `REAUTHORIZATION_TOO_SOON`: "A reauthorization is only allowed once from Day 4 to Day 29", `REAUTHORIZATION_NOT_SUPPORTED`: "A reauthorize cannot be attempted on an authorization_id that is the result of a prior reauthorization", and extend-authorization page: "Each authorization can be reauthorized only once." Sandbox evidence (Jaminan, Devpost): "on day 9 the sandbox reauthorized two holds … and refused a second attempt with 422 \"only allowed once\"" — https://devpost.com/software/jaminan. Treat as **once**.
- Reauthorize amount: "in US it is up to 115% of the original authorized amount, not to exceed an increase of $75 USD. Supports only the `amount` request parameter." — Payments schema. "A reauthorized payment itself has a new honor period of three days." Reauth returns a **new authorization ID**; capture against it (auth-honor page). `CANNOT_BE_VOIDED`: "A reauthorization cannot be voided. Please void the original parent authorization."
- Reauth inside honour period → 422 `REAUTHORIZATION_TOO_SOON` — Payments schema 422 example.
- Buyer side: "A hold on the money that is valid for 29 days" and "A `Pending` transaction in your business account" — https://developer.paypal.com/v5/checkout/auth-capture. "Buyers and merchants cannot close accounts that have authorized but uncaptured payments." — https://developer.paypal.com/payment-methods/auth-honor. "Reauthorization happens server-side without customer approval." — extend-authorization page.

## 3. Partial and multiple captures, `final_capture`

- `final_capture`: "Indicates whether you can make additional captures against the authorized payment. Set to `true` if you do not intend to capture additional payments against the authorization. Set to `false` if you intend to capture additional payments" — default `false` — Payments schema `capture_request`.
- Remainder release, v2 guide: "V1's default capture behavior voided the authorization after capture. V2's `final_capture` defaults to `false`. Without `true`, every v2 capture leaves the authorization open, causing unexpected balance holds on buyers' accounts. … Always set `final_capture: true` explicitly on every final capture call." — https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/integration/payments-api/v1-v2-migration (implies `true` closes the auth and releases the rest; the REST reference does not say "void" verbatim).
- NVP equivalent, explicit: "If `Complete`, any remaining amount of the original authorized transaction is automatically voided" — https://developer.paypal.com/api/nvp-soap/do-capture-nvp
- After a final capture: `AUTHORIZATION_ALREADY_CAPTURED` "If `final_capture` is set to to `true`, additional captures are not possible against the authorization." — Payments schema `x-errors`. Sandbox evidence: "$18.00 captured, $82.00 released, read-back CAPTURED, and a second capture refused with HTTP 422 AUTHORIZATION_ALREADY_CAPTURED" — https://devpost.com/software/jaminan
- Multiple captures: "Authorize the full order amount in a single request, then create multiple captures … by setting final_capture=\"false\"" and "You'll also need to provide a unique invoice_id for each capture." — https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/troubleshooting/rest_unprocessable_entity. Cap: `MAX_CAPTURE_COUNT_EXCEEDED` "Maximum number of allowable captures has been reached." (no number stated).
- Enablement: "Partial captures are supported during a single authorization period. For PayPal payment authorizations, you must enable partial capture on your PayPal account." — https://developer.paypal.com/payment-methods/auth-honor. How, and whether sandbox needs it: not stated **[UNVERIFIED]** (Jaminan's partial final capture worked in sandbox).
- Only one authorization per order: `ORDER_ALREADY_AUTHORIZED` "only one authorization is allowed per order … A merchant cannot authorize a partial amount of an order and authorize the remainder separately." — rest_unprocessable_entity page.
- AUTHORIZE with several purchase units: "This intent is not supported when you have more than one `purchase_unit` within your order." — Orders schema `checkout_payment_intent`.

## 4. Void and statuses

- "Voids, or cancels, an authorized payment, by ID. You cannot void an authorized payment that has been fully captured." Responses: `200` with `Prefer: return=representation`, else `204`; 422 `PREVIOUSLY_CAPTURED`; 409 `PREVIOUS_REQUEST_IN_PROGRESS` — Payments schema `POST …/void`.
- "All-or-nothing operation: You cannot partially void an authorization." / "In sandbox mode, funds release immediately. In production, funds can take up to 24 hours" — https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/void-authorized-payment
- Other void errors (x-errors): `PREVIOUSLY_VOIDED`, `CANNOT_BE_VOIDED` (reauth child), `AUTHORIZATION_VOIDED` ("cannot be captured or reauthorized"), `MULTIPLE_AUTHORIZATIONS_FOUND`.
- Authorization `status` enum (v2): `CREATED`, `CAPTURED`, `DENIED`, `PARTIALLY_CAPTURED`, `VOIDED`, `PENDING`; `status_details.reason` ∈ `PENDING_REVIEW`, `DECLINED_BY_RISK_FRAUD_FILTERS`; plus `expiration_time` — Payments + Orders schemas. **`EXPIRED` is not in the v2 enum**; expiry shows as `VOIDED` + webhook (§2). 422 `AUTHORIZATION_EXPIRED` exists for capture.
- Capture `status`: `COMPLETED`, `DECLINED`, `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`, `PENDING`, `REFUNDED`, `FAILED`; `status_details.reason` includes `RECEIVING_PREFERENCE_MANDATES_MANUAL_ACTION`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `UNILATERAL`, `VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`, `OTHER` — Payments schema. The schema's own empty-body capture example returns `"status": "PENDING", "status_details": {"reason": "OTHER"}`.

## 5. Orders v2 authorize flow, PATCH, breakdown

- Authorize endpoint: "To successfully authorize payment for an order, the buyer must first approve the order or a valid payment_source must be provided" — Orders schema `POST /v2/checkout/orders/{id}/authorize`. Its 422 issues: `PAYER_ACTION_REQUIRED`, `PAYMENT_DENIED`.
- Authorization ID path: "The authorization ID is in the order approval response at `purchase_units[0].payments.authorizations[0].id`." — https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/delay-capture (it is the `/authorize` response; the schema sample has `purchase_units[0].payments.authorizations[0]` with `id`, `status: CREATED`, `expiration_time`). Order `status` becomes `COMPLETED` after authorize.
- PATCH: "Updates an order with a `CREATED` or `APPROVED` status. You cannot update an order with the `COMPLETED` status." Patchable: `purchase_units[].amount` (replace), `purchase_units[].items` (replace, add, remove), `intent` (replace), `invoice_id`, `custom_id`, `description` — Orders schema `PATCH /v2/checkout/orders/{id}`. So the amount can change **after approval, before authorize**, not after (order is `COMPLETED`).
- "Capture a different total amount without the payer re-approving the order." and "If you update the final amount … after the payer approves … show a **Continue** button … add `commit=false`" and "Using `commit=false` reduces the number of payment methods … When possible, determine the final amount before the payer approves the transaction, and avoid using `PATCH`." — https://developer.paypal.com/expanded/update-order-details (v5 doc)
- How far a PATCH may raise an approved amount: not stated in the reference **[UNVERIFIED]**. (x-errors tie the 115% overage to "the sum of all captures … of the order amount".) PATCH 422 issue in schema: `PAYER_PATCH_NOT_ALLOWED`; `INVALID_PATCH_OPERATION` (add/replace/remove mismatches) — rest_unprocessable_entity page.
- Breakdown: "If you specify `amount.breakdown`, the amount equals `item_total` plus `tax_total` plus `shipping` plus `handling` plus `insurance` minus `shipping_discount` minus discount." `item_total`: "Required if the request includes `purchase_units[].items[].unit_amount`. Must equal the sum of `(items[].unit_amount * items[].quantity)`". `tax_total`: "Must equal the sum of `(items[].tax * items[].quantity)`" — Orders schema `amount_with_breakdown`, `amount_breakdown`. Errors: `AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, `ITEM_TOTAL_MISMATCH` — https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/integration/orders-api/troubleshooting

## 6. JS SDK

- v5: "add `&intent=authorize` to the JavaScript SDK script tag" — https://developer.paypal.com/v5/checkout/auth-capture. |---|---|---|---|

- Sandbox delivery: "subscribe it to a sandbox REST App's events … and generate actual test events with an integration that uses the app" and the listener must be "deployed at the corresponding URL that listens on HTTPS port 443" — https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/webhooks/rest. - **[DISCREPANCY]** self-verify string: `transmission_id|transmission_time|webhook_id|crc32` with "CRC32 … in decimal form … use the original raw body" (https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/webhooks/rest) vs. `transmission_id|transmission_time|webhook_id|webhook_body_hash` (https://developer.paypal.com/api/invoicing/webhooks). Use the REST guide (CRC32) or the postback.

## 8. Invoicing v2

- Create: "The `invoice` object must include an `items` array." Then "To move the invoice from a draft to payable state, you must send the invoice." — Invoicing schema `POST /v2/invoicing/invoices`.
- Send: `POST /v2/invoicing/invoices/{invoice_id}/send`, body `notification` (`send_to_invoicer`, `send_to_recipient`); "To send the invoice through a share link and not through PayPal, set the `send_to_recipient` parameter to `false`"; "After you send an invoice, resending it has no effect." Response `link_description` (202 example `rel: "payer-view"`, `href: …/invoice/p#INV2-…`) — Invoicing schema.
- QR: `POST /v2/invoicing/invoices/{invoice_id}/generate-qr-code`, optional body `{"width":150–500,"height":150–500,"action":"pay"|"details"}` (defaults 500, 500, `pay`). "The QR code is a PNG image in Base64-encoded format" and "Before you get a QR code, you must create an invoice and send an invoice" — Invoicing schema. Response content type: schema gives no media type; Postman lists `text/plain` — https://www.postman.com/paypal/paypal-public-api-workspace/request/jkpak9o/generate-qr-code **[UNVERIFIED]** exact body shape.
- Partial payments: `configuration.partial_payment.allow_partial_payment` (default `false`) + `minimum_amount_due`; "This feature is not available for users in `India`, `Brazil`, or `Israel`." — Invoicing schema `partial_payment`. Statuses include `PARTIALLY_PAID`, `PAYMENT_PENDING`, `MARKED_AS_PAID`. (A US sandbox merchant avoids the India exclusion.)
- Sandbox buyer payment: "Your webhook listener never receives the `INVOICING.INVOICE.PAID` event, even after an invoice is paid in the sandbox." (implies sandbox invoices are payable) — https://developer.paypal.com/invoicing/troubleshooting. The documented test path is "mark as paid" (`POST …/payments`) — https://developer.paypal.com/api/invoicing/webhooks. A sandbox personal account paying through the hosted page: **[UNVERIFIED]**, test it.
- Gotcha: "The `invoicer.email_address` … must exactly match the PayPal Business account associated with your access token." — https://developer.paypal.com/invoicing/troubleshooting
- **[DISCREPANCY]** status after send: "This moves the invoice from `DRAFT` to `SENT` status" and "The invoice status changes to `UNPAID`" (same page) — https://developer.paypal.com/api/invoicing. Lifecycle: `SENT` = emailed, `UNPAID` = shared by link or QR — https://developer.paypal.com/invoicing/lifecycle

## 9. Refunds

- Body: "For a full refund, include an empty payload … For a partial refund, include an `amount` object"; omitted amount refunds "`captured amount - previous refunds`"; optional `invoice_id`, `custom_id`, `note_to_payer` (≤255) — Payments schema `refund_request`.
- Idempotency: same `PayPal-Request-Id` → "200 OK response with the existing refund details" — Payments schema example `captures_refund_200_idempotent_response`. `DUPLICATE_REFUND`: "Requested invoice_id has been previously refunded."
- Window: "Process refunds within 180 days of the original capture date." — https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/refund-payment. Errors: `REFUND_TIME_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `REFUND_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED`, `CAPTURE_FULLY_REFUNDED`, `PENDING_CAPTURE` ("Cannot initiate a refund as the capture is pending"), `MAX_NUMBER_OF_REFUNDS_EXCEEDED`, `TRANSACTION_DISPUTED` — Payments schema. "In sandbox mode, refunds complete instantly." — refund-payment page.
- **[DISCREPANCY] idempotency retention** (existing notes say "up to 45 days"): v1-v2 guide: "`PayPal-Request-Id` (30-day window) … retention window reduced to **6 hours**" — https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/integration/payments-api/v1-v2-migration. Max length: idempotency page "the 38 single-byte character limit" (https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/reference/idempotency) vs. Payments schema `maxLength: 10000`. Keep UUIDs.

## 10. Sandbox gotchas (2025–2026)

- Void fails while capture works on the same AUTHORIZE order: `NOT_AUTHORIZED` "Authorization failed due to insufficient permissions." (SO, Dec 2, 2025, 1 answer, cause not confirmed) — https://stackoverflow.com/questions/79836030/paypal-void-authorization-fails-with-permission-denied-but-capture-works **[UNVERIFIED cause]**
- `COMPLIANCE_VIOLATION` on capture in sandbox (SO 2025; another for a Bosnia merchant, 2026) — https://stackoverflow.com/questions/79494485/paypal-api-error-the-requested-action-could-not-be-performed-semantically-inc, https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/paypal-sandbox?tab=Newest. Consistent with the existing note: non-US merchant → use US sandbox accounts.
- "Sandbox accounts registered in India fail at checkout, because PayPal doesn't process payments within India, so the demo uses US sandbox accounts." — https://devpost.com/software/holdfast-jgt83r
- Card-path authorize "answered 422 without a stated reason (we believe advanced card processing is not enabled for this Indonesia-region sandbox merchant)" — https://devpost.com/software/jaminan
- `PENDING` captures: the schema's own sample capture returns `PENDING` / `OTHER`; reasons include `RECEIVING_PREFERENCE_MANDATES_MANUAL_ACTION` (Payments schema). Refunds on a pending capture fail with `PENDING_CAPTURE`.
- `PAYEE_ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED`: "Contact PayPal customer support to lift restrictions on the receiving account." — https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/integration/orders-api/troubleshooting. No 2025–2026 sandbox report found.
- Simulator events: "Postback verification to the verify-webhook-signature endpoint is not supported for mock events" (already in notes).
- **Crowding signal, same hackathon**: Jaminan (rental deposit as a PayPal authorization: partial `final_capture`, void, reauthorize, invoice with QR; quotes the PayPal AI Hackathon brief) and Holdfast (freelancer hold: AUTHORIZE → partial capture with `final_capture: true` → Payouts) already use the hold-and-partial-capture primitive — https://devpost.com/software/jaminan, https://devpost.com/software/holdfast-jgt83r

- §2 idempotency "Retention varies (the refund example says up to 45 days)": add the v1-v2 guide's "6 hours" [DISCREPANCY].
